About Sentinel

Trust is earned with evidence.

Built for the IBM SkillBuild Hackathon, Sentinel explores a safer way to apply AI to software maintenance. Rather than treating a generated patch as a finished answer, the project emphasizes isolation, test evidence, transparent audit trails, and a person making the final call.

Safety and proof over blind automation.Our guiding principle
